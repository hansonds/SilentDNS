SilentDNS Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

SilentDNS is a free Android app made by Hanson ("I", "me") that lets you choose the DNS servers your phone uses. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: SilentDNS does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app does

When you turn SilentDNS on, it uses Android's VPN feature (VpnService) to receive only your phone's DNS lookups — the website and service names your apps ask for, such as example.com. It forwards each lookup to the primary or secondary DNS server you chose, and passes the answer back to your phone. All other internet traffic goes out normally and never passes through the app.

Information the app handles

  • DNS lookups. These are processed in your phone's memory only for as long as it takes to get an answer. They are not logged, saved, analysed or sent to me.
  • Your settings. The two DNS server addresses you enter and whether the app is on are saved on your phone only.
  • Counters. The "answered / encrypted / failed" numbers shown in the app are kept in memory and reset when the app is turned off.

The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party libraries. It never sends data to me or to any server other than the DNS servers you choose.

The DNS servers you choose

Your DNS lookups are sent to the DNS providers you select (for example Cloudflare at 1.1.1.1 or Google at 8.8.8.8). Where the provider supports it, lookups are sent encrypted using DNS-over-HTTPS. Those providers handle lookups under their own privacy policies, for example Cloudflare and Google Public DNS.

Sharing

I do not sell, share or transfer any user data, because the app does not collect any.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes it completely.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
